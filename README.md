# UNTRANSLATABLE

## 1. Project Name
**Untranslatable**

## 2. Project Idea

A satirical 2D motion graphics short film exploring the linguistic resilience and cultural identity of Egyptians in the face of colonial influence.

Through fast-paced comedic storytelling and dark satire, the project highlights how foreign powers attempted to impose their languages and cultures on Egypt, only to find that Egyptians naturally adapted, transformed, and reclaimed these influences in their own distinctive way.

The film celebrates the adaptability, humor, and enduring spirit of Egyptian identity through a creative blend of visual storytelling and cultural commentary.
